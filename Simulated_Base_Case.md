| Study | Simulation / usable data |
|---|---|
| [Zhang et al., 2023 — *Ultra-thermostable embedded liquid cooling in SiC 3D packaging power modules of electric vehicles*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2022.116499) | Embedded non-uniform micro-pin-fin liquid cooler in a SiC substrate; numerical and experimental validation. At 100 mL/min water flow, reported stable 320 W dissipation, 78.89% lower junction-to-coolant resistance vs. DBC, and 68.69% better temperature uniformity. |
| [Tang et al., 2024 — *Integrated Thermal Management for a High-Power-Density SiC Power Module With Die-Level Heat Flux Over 1000 W/cm²*](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10818454/) | Realistic thermal-flow simulations plus experiments for an embedded manifold-microchannel SiC module. Reported 1,191 W/cm² die heat flux at 0.192 L/min, 64.1 °C average die temperature rise, and 117.2 kPa pressure drop for a 2×2 array. |
| [Sun et al., 2025 — *A Novel Substrate-Embedded SiC Power Module With Integrated Liquid Cooling*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPEL.2025.3563077) | Transient thermal simulation of four embedded 1.2-kV SiC MOSFETs on a direct-bonded microchannel heat sink. The 20 × 20 × 2.4 mm module exceeded TO-247-4 die heat flux by 29.1% against air cooling and 7.6% against external liquid cooling at a 150 °C junction-temperature rise. |
| [Huo et al., 2026 — *Two-Phase Flow Boiling for Ultra-High Heat Flux SiC Chip Cooling*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0017931025012207) | Numerical heat-transfer models of an embedded SiC heat sink under single- and two-phase flows. This is the most directly relevant recent simulation-only lead, but the accessible record did not expose its numerical result tables. |
